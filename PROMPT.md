# Clean Master Prompt

Act as a professional bilingual Arabic/English website builder, UX/UI designer, Dubai real estate trainer, and compliance-aware real estate content strategist.

Build a premium interactive HTML website called **Dubai Real Estate Broker Starter Kit** for new real estate brokers in Dubai.

## Brand

- Collaboration: SMT Institute × Al Hamoudi Real Estate
- Style: luxury Dubai, black, charcoal, ivory, soft gold accents
- UI: clean, animated, mobile-first, elegant, easy to digest
- Language: Arabic and English with a visible language switcher
- Tone: simple, practical, beginner-friendly, professional, not salesy

## Website sections

1. Hero with Dubai skyline feeling and collaboration logos
2. Summary by categories
3. Official Dubai market snapshot using DLD / Dubai Government sources
4. Ecosystem explainer: DLD, RERA, Trakheesi, Dubai REST, Ejari, Oqood, Escrow, Form A, Form B, Form F, Title Deed, NOC
5. Deal flow: buyer, seller, off-plan, rental, daily broker routine
6. Deal calculator: DLD fee, trustee fee, title deed, map fee, commission, optional mortgage fee
7. Developer guide with USP, best-fit client, examples, and watch-outs
8. Hidden-gem areas with opportunity and risk notes
9. Buyer nationalities/personas with clear disclaimer where data is market intelligence, not official DLD nationality fields
10. Broker mistakes and failures
11. AI tool stack for brokers
12. Glossary of broker keywords and abbreviations
13. First 30-days checklist with progress saving
14. Quick quiz
15. Sources and accuracy notes

## UX requirements

- Use only static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript
- No paid libraries
- Ready for GitHub Pages
- Responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Accessible contrast, keyboard-friendly buttons, semantic HTML
- Smooth animations with reduced-motion support
- Store language and checklist progress in localStorage

## Accuracy rules

- Use official DLD/Dubai Government sources for market numbers and fees
- Mark unofficial nationality trends as market intelligence, not official transaction-level data
- Do not promise guaranteed ROI
- Explain risks alongside opportunities
- Keep wording simple and practical
