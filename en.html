ECOSYSTEM

Who is responsible for what?

DLD

Dubai Land Department, the main government authority for the sector.

RERA

Regulatory arm associated with broker and developer activity.

Trakheesi

Platform associated with real estate advertising and activity permits.

Oqood

System associated with registration of certain off-plan contracts.

Ejari

Dubai tenancy registration system.

NOC

No Objection Certificate that may be required in certain transfers.
